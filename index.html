Open translation on Google ↗

Text is sent to Google Translate only when you open the link. Copy the result on Google and paste it back into the writer if needed.

▧

Translate text inside images

Google Translate accepts JPG, PNG and WebP images. Open Google, choose Images, then select your image on Google’s website. Google may let you copy translated text or download the translated image.

Open Google image translation ↗

For your privacy, e2t does not read or upload your selected images. Any image you upload to Google is processed under Google’s terms.

▤

Translate Word, PDF & Office files

Open Google Translate’s Documents mode and select a .docx, .pdf, .pptx or .xlsx file there. Supported documents are up to 10 MB; PDFs may have up to 300 pages. Google offers a translated document to download.

Open Google document translation ↗

Google’s document translator is not available on small screens. Text inside scanned PDF pages is not translated. Your files are uploaded directly to Google, not through e2t.

↗

Translate a website

Open Google Translate’s Websites mode, paste the website URL there, and start translating. Availability depends on Google’s region and browser support.

Open Google website translation ↗

This follows the op=websites mode from your example link. Text translation uses op=translate instead.

Independent integration: Google Translate is operated by Google. e2t links to its public website, rather than using an undocumented translation API. Translations and uploads happen on Google; your locally saved writer draft is never shared automatically.
